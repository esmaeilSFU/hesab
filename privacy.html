Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: Hesab never sees your money. Your entries, budgets and categories stay on your phone, or in a Google Sheet in your Google Drive if you choose to sync. The developer only receives anonymous usage counts, and your email only if you choose to give it.

1. Your budget data

  • On this device (default). Everything is stored in your browser's storage on your phone. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app or clearing website data removes it, so use Save full backup in Settings.
  • Google Sheet sync (optional). If you sign in with Google, Hesab asks for one permission: access to the Google Drive files you choose or that Hesab creates (the drive.file scope). Hesab cannot see any other file in your Drive, your email, or your contacts. Your entries travel directly between your phone and Google. They never pass through a Hesab server, because there isn't one.
  • Joining a budget someone shared with you. Only in this case, Hesab also asks to open your Google Sheets (the spreadsheets scope), because the narrow permission above can't open a sheet another person shared. Hesab uses it only to open the shared budget sheet you joined; it never reads or changes your other sheets.
  • Budgets you joined are also noted in a small file Hesab creates in your own Google Drive (“Hesab · shared budgets.json”: the shared sheet's id and name), so Hesab on your other screens can offer them. You can delete it any time.
  • Sharing your budget. When you invite someone in Hesab, Hesab asks Google to share your budget sheet with that email address, and Google emails them the invitation. You can remove their access in Hesab or in Google Drive.
  • Shopping lists are stored on your phone and, if you use a Google Sheet, in a “Lists” tab of that sheet so everyone who shares the budget sees them. They are included in backups you save yourself.
  • While you're signed in, the Google access token is kept only for the open app session and is cleared when you sign out.
  • You can remove Hesab's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2. Use of Google user data

Hesab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Sheet is used only to show and update your budget inside the app on your device. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

3. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn how many people use Hesab, the app sends a small anonymous record at most once a day and when it is added to a Home Screen:

  • a random device number created by the app (not linked to your name or account)
  • event type (for example "opened" or "installed"), platform (iPhone, Android or computer) and app version
  • where your data is kept (on the device or Google Sheet) and your time zone

It never includes amounts, categories, notes, your Google account or your sheet. You can turn this off in Settings → Updates & privacy.

4. Your email (optional)

If you choose to enter your email in Settings, it is stored by the developer and used only to send occasional news about Hesab. It is never sold or shared. To have it removed, write to the support address below.

5. Where the developer's data is kept

Anonymous usage records and emails are stored in a Google Form and Google Sheet owned by the developer. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Children

Hesab is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions or requests: hesab-app-support@googlegroups.com.

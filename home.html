Questions

Is Hesab free?

Yes. Hesab is free to use.

Do I need a Google account?

No. You can keep everything on your phone. A Google account is only needed if you want your budget in a Google Sheet, synced between phones.

Does Hesab connect to my bank?

No. You log spending yourself, which takes a few seconds and keeps you aware of where the money goes. Your bank login never touches Hesab.

Can my partner and I share one budget?

Yes. In Settings → Share this budget, enter their email and choose “Can edit” or “Can view”. Google emails them an invitation; the link in it opens Hesab and joins the budget, shopping lists included.

How do I stop Hesab from accessing my Google account?

Sign out in Hesab's Settings, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your sheet stays in your Drive.
